🎯 What Is MacTV?

MacTV is a beautifully simple macOS app that lives in your menu bar and gives you complete control over your TV when it's connected to your Mac. Instead of fumbling with a separate remote control, MacTV lets you:

  • Adjust volume directly from your Mac
  • Switch input sources (HDMI 1, HDMI 2, etc.) with one click
  • Navigate TV menus using your Mac's keyboard and trackpad
  • Power your TV on/off right from your desktop

All of this works through HDMI-CEC — a standard feature built into most modern TVs and HDMI cables. No extra hardware needed. No complicated setup. Just plug in your Mac and TV via HDMI, install MacTV, and you're ready to go.

🖥️ System Requirements

  • Mac model: Any Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)
  • macOS version: macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • TV: Any TV that supports HDMI-CEC (also called Anynet+, Bravia Sync, Simplink, or similar)
  • Cable: A standard HDMI cable connected between your Mac and TV
💡 Tip: Most TVs from Samsung, LG, Sony, TCL, Vizio, and other major brands support HDMI-CEC. Look for "CEC," "HDMI Control," or "Bravia Sync" in your TV's settings and make sure it's enabled.

🚀 Getting Started

Getting MacTV running takes less than five minutes. Here's exactly what to do:

Step 1: Connect your Mac to your TV

Plug an HDMI cable from your Mac into any HDMI port on your TV. Make sure the TV is turned on and set to the correct input.

Step 2: Enable HDMI-CEC on your TV

Using your TV's remote, go to the TV's settings menu and find the HDMI-CEC option. It might be called "Anynet+", "Bravia Sync", "Simplink", "Viera Link", or something similar. Turn it on.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Most issues are easy to fix. Here are some common problems and solutions:

TV not responding to MacTV

Make sure HDMI-CEC is enabled on your TV. Also, try unplugging and replugging the HDMI cable, and restart both your Mac and TV.

Volume doesn't work

Some TVs only allow volume control when the TV's speakers are active. If you're using external speakers connected to the TV, volume control via CEC may not work.

MacTV doesn't open

Check that you're using macOS 13 or later and that you have an Apple Silicon Mac. If you downloaded the app to your Downloads folder, try right-clicking it and selecting "Open" the first time to bypass macOS security warnings.
